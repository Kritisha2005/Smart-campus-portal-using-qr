📖 About the Library

The Central Library of Kanpur Institute of Technology supports students and faculty in academic learning, research and knowledge development.

✨ Library Facilities

  • Textbooks and reference books
  • Academic journals and magazines
  • Newspapers and reading materials
  • Quiet reading and self-study space
  • Digital learning resources, where available

📚 Available Resources

  • Engineering and Technology
  • Computer Science and IT
  • Management
  • Science and Mathematics
  • General Knowledge

🕒 Library Timings

  • Day Scholar Students: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Hostel Students: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM

📋 Library Rules

  1. Maintain silence inside the library.
  2. Carry your valid college ID card.
  3. Keep books in good condition.
  4. Return books by the due date.
  5. Follow library staff instructions.

🎓 Book Issue and Return Policy

  • Students can issue books for 1 month.
  • Books must be returned on or before the due date.
  • Students can request reissue of books.
  • Books not returned or reissued on time are subject to a fine.

💰 Late Return Fine

A fine of ₹5 per day is charged for each day a book is overdue without return or reissue.

💡 Why Visit the Library?

  • Prepare for semester examinations.
  • Improve subject knowledge.
  • Find reference material for projects.
  • Study in a peaceful environment.

📞 Library Help

For library-related queries, visit the Central Library or contact the college administration.
